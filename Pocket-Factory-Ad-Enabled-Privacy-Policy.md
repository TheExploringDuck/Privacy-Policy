# Pocket Factory Privacy Policy

Developer: TheExploringDuck

Effective date: October 5, 2026

App: Pocket Factory (com.theexploringduck.pocketfactory)

## Scope

This policy applies to versions of Pocket Factory that include optional Google AdMob advertising, including test advertising in development and testing builds. Earlier offline-only versions do not include AdMob. Pocket Factory does not require an account. The developer does not operate a server that receives your local game saves or maintains player profiles.

## Game data on your device

Pocket Factory stores progress, resource balances, equipment and production-line levels, supervisor and progression state, visual preferences, introduction completion, and a timestamp for offline production locally on your device. Local diagnostic logs may also be created. These files let you resume playing and troubleshoot problems. Local saves are not uploaded to the developer or used as advertising profiles.

New Game creates a local backup before restarting progress. It does not completely delete your data. To remove local saves, settings, backups, and logs on Android, open device Settings, find Pocket Factory, and clear its app storage/data. Uninstalling normally removes app-local data as well. Removing this data permanently removes progress from that installation. Device transfers, manual copies, or other operating-system features may retain separate copies. The app disables Android automatic app cloud backup and has no developer-operated cloud save. We cannot remotely delete files stored only on your device.

## Optional advertising

Ad-enabled versions use Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Players choose whether to watch a rewarded ad for a clearly stated temporary benefit. Normal progression does not require watching ads. The game does not show forced interstitial, app-open, or banner advertisements.

Google's advertising SDK may process your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or account identifiers, interactions with ads or the app, and performance or diagnostic information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure advertising, operate its services, and prevent abuse. The information transmitted depends on SDK configuration, device settings, and applicable privacy choices. Restricted ad treatment is configured for this 13+ game; advertising-identifier transmission and personalized advertising are restricted by those settings. Test ads still involve communication with Google's services. We do not claim that test or restricted ads collect no data.

The game requests updated privacy information through Google's User Messaging Platform before initializing advertising. Where required by the service, a privacy form is shown and an Ad Choices control allows you to revisit your choices. If privacy setup or an ad fails, the ordinary game remains available without that optional reward.

Google handles advertising information under its Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google's SDK data disclosure guidance: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure

## Permissions and other services

Ads require an internet connection and network access. Pocket Factory does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise device location. Approximate location inferred from an IP address is different from GPS access. Advertising SDKs can declare additional permissions through the Android manifest; your Android privacy controls and the SDK's restricted settings affect their use.

The game has no separate developer-operated analytics, crash-reporting, online leaderboard, in-app billing, or account service. Advertising diagnostics are part of Google's SDK. Google Play and Android may independently process downloads, updates, reviews, and app-quality information according to their policies and your settings. Google Play can make feedback and app-quality reports available to the developer.

This policy may be hosted on GitHub or GitHub Pages. GitHub processes requests to its websites under its Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Our standalone policy page contains no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels.

## Support information

If you email us or submit a bug report, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address, message, device model, app version, screenshots, or diagnostic details. We use it to respond, investigate problems, and improve the game. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Email and support providers process communications to deliver and store messages. Public reviews and GitHub issues are visible to others and governed by those platforms. Use email for personal information rather than a public issue.

## Sharing, retention, and requests

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising information is processed by Google as described above. Information you provide through support may be disclosed when necessary to meet a legal obligation or protect users and the service. This does not give us access to saves stored only on your device.

Support messages are retained only as needed to address your request, handle related follow-up, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can email us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you sent us. We may verify the request and retain information where required by law. There is no Pocket Factory account to delete.

Information independently processed by Google, GitHub, or another platform is subject to that provider's retention and deletion practices. Manage Google account and Android advertising privacy settings through those services; we cannot directly delete their independently held records.

## Security

The game uses device app storage for local files. We do not claim that save files are separately encrypted or that any storage method is completely secure. Keep your device protected and avoid publishing sensitive information in feedback.

## Audience

Pocket Factory is intended for ages 13 and older. The developer does not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information to us through support, email us to request its deletion. A 13+ audience is not treated as proof that all players are adults; restricted advertising settings apply to this build.

## Policy changes

We will update this policy and relevant store disclosures when features or data practices change. The effective date identifies this revision. Any future purchases, accounts, online features, or other advertising configurations will require appropriate disclosures before use.
