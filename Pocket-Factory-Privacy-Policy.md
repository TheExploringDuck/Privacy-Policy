# Pocket Factory Privacy Policy

**Developer:** TheExploringDuck  
**Effective date:** October 5, 2026  
**App:** Pocket Factory (`com.theexploringduck.pocketfactory`)

## About this policy

This policy describes the current offline versions of Pocket Factory. The game does not require an account and does not send gameplay or personal information to servers operated by TheExploringDuck. There is currently no integrated advertising service, in-app billing service, analytics SDK, developer-operated crash-reporting service, online leaderboard, or cloud-save service.

## Information stored on your device

Pocket Factory stores game progress and settings locally so you can resume playing. This includes resource balances, equipment and production-line levels, supervisor and progression state, visual preferences, introduction completion, and a save timestamp used to calculate offline production. The game may also write local diagnostic logs to help identify errors.

Versions with a New Game option create a local backup of the previous save before starting over. These backups are not uploaded to us.

The current Android release does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or advertising identifier. Its game functionality does not require the Android Internet permission. We do not use the game to track you across other apps or websites.

## Information you choose to send us

If you contact us or send a bug report, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address, message, device model, app version, screenshots, or diagnostic details. We use this information to respond, investigate problems, and improve the game. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Our email or support providers process communications to deliver and store those messages. Posts you choose to make in public GitHub issues or public store reviews are visible to others and are also governed by that platform's policies. Use private support contact for personal information, not a public issue.

## Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. The current game does not transmit your local save or gameplay activity to us or an advertising or analytics provider.

Google Play and Android may independently process information associated with downloading, updating, running, or reviewing the app, including platform diagnostics, according to your settings and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google Play may make app-quality reports and feedback available to the developer. This is separate from an in-game analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

When you visit this policy on GitHub or GitHub Pages, GitHub processes website requests under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Our standalone policy page does not embed advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels.

We may disclose information you send through support where necessary to meet a legal obligation or protect users and the service. This does not give us access to saves stored only on your device.

## Retention and deletion

Local progress, settings, backups, and diagnostic files remain on your device until removed. **New Game restarts progress but is not a complete data-deletion feature: it retains a backup.** To remove the app's local data on Android, use your device's Settings, find Pocket Factory, and choose the option to clear app storage/data. Uninstalling normally removes app-local data as well. This permanently removes progress from that installation.

The current Android build disables Android's automatic app cloud backup and does not offer its own cloud sync. Device-transfer tools, manually copied files, or other operating-system features may retain separate copies; manage those through the relevant service or device settings. We cannot remotely delete files stored only on your device.

We retain support messages only as needed to address the request, handle related follow-up, and satisfy applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you provided to us. We may need to verify the request and may retain information where required by law. There is no Pocket Factory account to delete.

## Security

The game uses the device's app storage for local saves. We do not claim that save files are separately encrypted or that any storage method is completely secure. Keep your device and operating system protected and avoid including sensitive information in public feedback.

## Children

The current game does not request personal information from players or include in-game chat, accounts, or advertising. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information to us through support can contact us to request its deletion.

## Future features and policy changes

Advertising, purchases, competition, and online features may be added in the future, but they are not active data-collection services in the current game. Before introducing features that change how information is handled, we will update this policy and the relevant store disclosures and provide any notices or choices required for those features. The effective date above identifies this policy's current revision.
